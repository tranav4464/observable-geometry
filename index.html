Observable Geometry

Inspect when distant candidate sources have similar scalp patterns under a declared EEG forward model.

What it does

Given a leadfield, matching noise covariance, source coordinates and model metadata, Observable Geometry ranks distant source pairs by the similarity of their predicted scalp patterns. It helps make model-conditioned ambiguity visible before interpreting a source estimate.

What the result means

A high score means two candidate sources produce similar modeled sensor patterns for the supplied forward model and covariance. It is not a probability that either source was active and does not establish recovery from a measured neural event.

The similarity formula is established mathematics. The project does not claim a novel score, calibrated source-localization uncertainty, or demonstrated benefit over standard resolution measures.
