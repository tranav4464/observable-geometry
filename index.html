What it does

Input a verified forward model, noise covariance, source coordinates and provenance. The tool compares noise-whitened patterns and ranks distant alternatives for each source. Export a ranked CSV, numerical scores, summary and audit trail.

Use it to inspect model ambiguity before interpreting a source estimate. The pairwise diagnostic is separate from the reproduced GBF inverse workflow.

Scope of acceptance

B8 passed for the bounded TKS software snapshot. External human review is deferred. This build does not establish endogenous neural-source recovery, a novel mathematical method or improved researcher decisions.

WSGZP geometry verification and measured-source validation are future extensions. The portable bundle excludes licensed model inputs and recordings; use your own verified model or the existing local demonstration inputs.
