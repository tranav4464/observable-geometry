# B4 alternate public dataset screen

**Screen date:** 24 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Determine whether an already public real dataset can reopen B4 for EEG source-pattern distinguishability under an individual forward model.  
**Data boundary:** Metadata and published descriptions only. No neural-signal samples were opened for this screen.

## Decision

**No screened alternative presently reopens B4–B6 as an independent confirmatory evaluation.** One important candidate, the public iES-CCEP dataset, has participant anatomy and stimulation-site information for source-imaging work. However, the 2026 GBF paper already reports localization results for 318 sessions from 35 participants from this data route, and the OSF API currently exposes no explicit dataset license. It could support a carefully labelled secondary analysis of a different frozen estimand, but not independent confirmation of GBF localization. This is a feasibility and prior-art decision, not evidence that no other qualifying dataset exists.

## Requirements for a qualifying dataset

To test whether the current GBF distinguishability output predicts a useful real-data outcome, the dataset must provide, for the same participant and acquisition:

1. scalp EEG with sensor positions, channel names/order, units, reference, and preprocessing provenance;
2. a participant-specific forward model or the MRI, tissue surfaces, transforms, electrode positions, and conductivity information needed to recreate and verify one;
3. an independent, spatially registered reference appropriate to the outcome, such as intracranial contacts and event timing, while acknowledging that SEEG samples only implanted locations;
4. enough independently repeated events/conditions and participants for the proposed split and unit of inference;
5. public access, license, and data-use terms that permit the proposed reproducible analysis; and
6. a task-level prior-art comparison showing what new decision the GBF output would add beyond existing ESI, resolution, source-separability, and EEG–SEEG comparison methods.

Availability of scalp EEG and intracranial EEG somewhere in a cohort is not sufficient: the pairing, geometry, reference, independent unit, and intended outcome must all be verified.

For a clinical source-localization outcome, a paired intracranial recording is not the only possible reference. A prespecified comparison to a resection cavity and postsurgical outcome can be informative, but it is an indirect clinical proxy for the epileptogenic zone, not direct observation of the complete source. Such a route needs patient-level separation, explicit definitions of concordance, and careful treatment of outcome and clinical-selection limitations.

## Prior-art implications

The alternatives reinforce rather than remove the novelty constraint. EEG–SEEG translation and source imaging already have direct literature, including the 2026 GBF paper's own 318-session stimulation-site localization evaluation. The iES-CCEP archive is a promising geometry-rich resource, but its broad GBF localization result is already public. A secondary question about incremental predictive value of the frozen B2 score could be distinct from that paper's inverse-method comparison, but the result would be post-publication and the public dataset license is not explicit in the OSF API metadata. A dataset alone would not make the GBF cosine a new method. Any reopening needs a distinct, testable decision, matched established comparators, clear data-use terms, and careful treatment of the stimulation site as a reference for the evoked measurement rather than complete ground truth for all neural sources.

The HDEEG-IED-SurgOutcome release is a newly identified candidate with participant MRI, electrode coordinates, EEG averages, postsurgical imaging, and outcome labels. It may support a participant-held-out, post-publication secondary evaluation of a fixed diagnostic against clinical localization outcomes. However, the GBF paper already reports results on 24 favourable-outcome participants from this same release. Any held-out route must verify exact participant overlap, retain the existing GBF analysis as prior art, establish the data-specific access terms, and lock its estimand and comparators before opening outcomes. The surgical endpoint is a clinical proxy for the epileptogenic zone and must not be described as direct source ground truth.

The 2025 Hao et al. study is also close to the broader empirical direction: it reports simultaneous 64-channel scalp EEG and SEEG, uses SEEG-informed source imaging, and evaluates localization distance. Its associated DRE-LZSH data are now publicly available through Zenodo and NEMAR, correcting the earlier screen that no public DOI had been identified. The release is described as an SEEG benchmark; its README reports scalp-labelled channels mixed into the recordings, but no electrode coordinates, unspecified units, and no event timing. The NEMAR participant table reports MRI status codes rather than released participant MRI. It may support a separate signal-prediction task, but it does not satisfy the individual-geometry contract for the frozen GBF source-distinguishability estimand.

## Gate consequences

- **B4:** Remains **PIVOT** for independent empirical validation of endogenous source distinguishability. The Localize-MI artifact branch remains closed to confirmatory outcomes. The iES-CCEP archive may support a secondary analysis of a distinct score-to-error estimand, but the 2026 GBF paper already analyzes nearly all of its sessions for localization, and data-use terms need clarification before signal access. This screen does not authorize signal access.
- **B5:** Remains **PIVOT**. The HDEEG-IED-SurgOutcome release is a plausible secondary-evaluation candidate, but its EBRAINS terms, overlap with the published GBF subset, precise score-to-outcome estimand, and independent protocol review have not been resolved. No outcome access is authorized by this metadata screen.
- **B6:** Remains **PIVOT**. No held-out outcome analysis was performed for these candidates.
- **B7:** Remains **PIVOT** for researcher decision utility. Model-only association with MNE PSF summaries is descriptive and cannot establish decision benefit.
- **B8:** This screen is additional provenance for the release packet. It does not replace the outstanding unaffiliated external review or independent reproduction.

## Reopening checklist

Reopen the empirical branch only after a candidate has a versioned public data record and license, a metadata-only pairing/geometry audit, adequate independent units for a prespecified split, a precise outcome and decision, a matched prior-art review, and independent technical review of a frozen protocol. If no candidate meets these conditions, finish the bounded reproduction and state plainly that no valid empirical test or novel method was established.
