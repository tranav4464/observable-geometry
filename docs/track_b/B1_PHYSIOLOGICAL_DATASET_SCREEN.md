# B1 physiological validation dataset screen

> **Historical screen.** This document was written for an earlier spatial source-confusion question. Its candidate ranking and dataset interpretation are not the active Track B task specification. The current decision is in [`B1_TASK_AND_DATA_DECISION.md`](B1_TASK_AND_DATA_DECISION.md); the active project uses real recordings only and is auditing on004752 for a bounded held-out prediction endpoint.

**Updated 2026-10-22.** This screen asks whether an available dataset can validate the proposed spatial source-confusion estimand. A dataset with paired scalp and intracranial recordings is not automatically spatial ground truth: the scalp signal is a mixture, and iEEG samples only implanted contacts.

## Direct metadata audit: OSF 89ndr

The successful allowlisted audit used the OSF file-provider endpoint after the public download endpoint returned HTTP 429. It fetched 107 metadata payloads and verified ZIP CRCs and SHA-256 hashes. It did **not** read EEG/SEEG arrays or NIfTI images. The machine-readable report is `outputs/track_b/data_acquisition/osf_89ndr_archive_metadata.json`; attempt status and scope are recorded in `outputs/track_b/data_acquisition/osf_89ndr_metadata_payload_audit_status.json`.

The metadata show 256 EEG channels and XYZ values for each participant, but only one iEEG channel/contact per participant. EEG coordinates are stated in T1w space, millimetres; iEEG coordinates are stated in native T1w space in metres, with a separate MNI coordinate table. Coordinate presence and stated units were checked, not registration accuracy or anatomical placement against MRI. EEG coordinate sidecars point to `sub-XX_T1.nii`, while the archive contains `sub-XX_T1w.nii`; this systematic `IntendedFor` mismatch must be reconciled before any forward model.

Each EEG and iEEG event table contains the same subject-specific row count: 71, 36, 18, 137, 73, 151, 71 and 103 (660 total). The saved summary confirms row counts and column names, but does not summarize the actual `trial_type` values. The sidecars describe “Stimulation of spike” and mark baseline correction false, whereas the 2024 paper describes IED recordings. This discrepancy is unresolved. Therefore the metadata audit verifies file payload integrity and structure, not event semantics, signal synchronization, signal quality or physiological ground truth.

## Prior art and decision

The 2024 [Zauli et al. study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneumeth.2024.110193) already performs electrical source imaging on simultaneous HD-EEG/SEEG IED data. The September 2026 [Matsubara et al. preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.25.26364055) uses six participants from this release to classify the 80-ms temporal bin of an IED and predict one selected contact's waveform. Its reported temporal accuracy varies from 0.080 to 0.984 and exceeds chance in three of six participants; mean waveform correlation is 0.752 for the model and 0.754 for the patient-template baseline. The preprint's “where” is temporal localization, not cortical spatial localization. Still, it occupies broad scalp-to-intracranial event/waveform framing on this cohort.

Clinical outcome is not a clean escape from prior art. The 2026 [Abdallah et al. study](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi.70347) evaluates EMSI spatial overlap, distance localization error and spatial dispersion against surgical cavities and outcomes in 25 patients. The 2025 44-patient cohort also explicitly supports EEG source-localization comparisons against resection and 12-month outcome. These studies make generic “uncertainty maps improve clinical localization” an inadequate novelty claim.

**B1 remains open, with a no-go on the current spatial pairwise validation route.** Zauli 89ndr fails the exact within-subject pair requirement. on004752 has suitable paired modalities and task labels for a different, bounded reanalysis, but the same cohort/task already supports published work on working-memory load and scalp-source/hippocampal information flow. A new project would need an exact estimand beyond those analyses, leakage-safe participant-held-out evaluation, and a credible comparison; it still could not claim individualized forward-ensemble identifiability or full source localization. Do not begin model fitting or claim novelty until that task survives B0 and a file-level on004752 audit. No signals have been inspected and no model has been trained.

The additional NeurIPS 2025 state-space paper raises the collision further: it already jointly estimates latent regional activity and directed connectivity from simultaneous scalp EEG/iEEG and reports working-memory phase comparisons on these participants. A replication or a tightly scoped challenge of its assumptions is possible; a generic multimodal whole-brain network claim is not. See [the equation and experiment audit](B0_WBEN_NEURIPS_2025_AUDIT.md).
