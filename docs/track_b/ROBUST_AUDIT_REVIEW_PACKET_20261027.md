## Exact claim to review

For each of two distinct fixed synthetic 20 nAm scalp fields (targets 2075 and 6103), the declared sign-coherent, density-capped two-window alternative family on one sub-01 model gives target-only fit at the nominal 0.009 S/m forward and illustrative `tau=1`. A separately generated 0.012 S/m forward inside the declared `[0.006,0.012]` S/m conductivity set gives a target minimum residual above 1 in both cases, refuting a **universal target-only statement** over that set. The current continuous forward radius is far too loose to prove positive alternative exclusion over the interval. The software reports `refuted_by_in_set_scenario` rather than `certified_target_only`. These are two targets on one participant model, not independent-participant replication.

## Known limitations and failure route

The conductivity interval and `tau=1` are illustrative stress assumptions. The family fixes sign per patch, density cap, total moment budget, source orientation and one cortical geometry. The interval calculation treats stored finite geometric factors and whitening as fixed; it does not enclose their assembly error or physical-head uncertainty. A direct endpoint refit already reveals the negative finding, so no novel inference algorithm is claimed. The six other Localize-MI forward files have not passed participant-specific covariance and source-model validation. No recorded EEG samples or outcomes were used in this audit. If any input-bound, source-correspondence or numerical-replay check fails, withdraw the robust-decision wording and report a failed or unresolved audit.
