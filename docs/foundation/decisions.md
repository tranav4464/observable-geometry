## Corrected decisions

- Withdraw any promise of a guaranteed breakthrough, publication, lab placement or full brain reconstruction.
- A method that reports uncertainty or runs SVD is not automatically new.
- REA is an investigation aid if a relevant opaque target exists, not a compulsory scientific component.
- Keep covariance and reference conventions consistent; a new average-reference montage cannot be treated as one extra independent row.
- Respect the data repository's CC-BY-NC-SA terms, not the article's CC-BY terms.
- Do not mistake an annex-pointer checkout for downloaded EEG.
- Full upstream GBFs replication requires assets beyond the source clone.

## What needs expert input later

The appropriate stimulation-source representation; credible head-conductivity and electrode-location uncertainty ranges; whether the proposed spatial confidence construction is justified for this dataset; and which comparison best distinguishes the method from existing robust inverse/design work. These questions can be sent to a lab only after you authorize outreach.

## Research status language

Use: "prototype", "conditional estimate", "candidate contribution", "development case" and "held-out result" when appropriate. Use "new framework" only after a concrete prior-art difference and evidence exist. Use "calibrated" only with reported calibration data and conditions. An engineering demo can be complete while the research claim remains unresolved.
